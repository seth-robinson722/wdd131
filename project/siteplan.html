Am I Being Scammed?

The Three Golden Rules

1. Ownership

Your "Seed Phrase" is the key to your vault. If you share it with anyone, they can take your money. Never type it on a website.

2. Verification

Scammers often pretend to be "Support" or celebrities. Official companies will never DM you first to ask for money or keys.

3. The Undo Button

In crypto, there is no "Undo." Always send a small $1 test transaction before moving large amounts of money. Slow is safe.

2026 Scam Spotter

If you see these "Red Flags," stop immediately:

  • ✅ Urgency: They say you must act "within 10 minutes" to save your account.
  • ✅ Too Good to Be True: Promising to "double your Bitcoin" if you send it to them first.
  • ✅ The Romantic Angle: Someone you met online starts giving you "investment advice."
